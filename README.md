# Leakwatch

**Find the ad money you're leaking.** Drop your Meta Ads Manager report. In 10 seconds you'll know
what to pause, what to fix, and what it saves you, in plain words.

Built for small businesses and freelancers who spend $300 to $5,000 a month on ads and can't
justify a $99/month tool. Open models explain every leak; your file is read in your browser.

> Status: in progress for the [Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge, Week 2: Side Hustle](https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12).
